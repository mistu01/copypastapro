## 📋 Release Notes - Mistus Copy Pasta (v1.3.25)

### 📌 Right-Aligned Pinned Items & Up to 5 Pins in Floating Bar
- **Pinned Items Positioned on the Right**:
  - In the desktop floating pill bar, pinned items are now placed on the **RIGHT** side of the chip bar, while unpinned recent items sit on the **LEFT** side.
- **Up to 5 Pinned Items Without Entry-Count Restriction**:
  - Removed the restriction where enabled entry count determined the maximum number of pinnable items.
  - Users can now pin **up to 5 items** directly in the desktop floating bar regardless of whether 3, 5, or 10 entries are enabled.
- **Fix for Clear History Confirmation Dialog**:
  - Resolved an issue where the clear unpinned history confirmation dialog was hidden behind the main window (only visible in taskbar hover).
  - The dialog now reliably stays on top centered directly over the main window on the realtime desktop with fluent Cyber-Emerald dark styling.
