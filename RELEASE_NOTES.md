### What's Changed in v1.3.37
- **Full Dark Mode Fix**: Eliminated white backgrounds inside scrollable tabs (`Clipboard`, `Pinned`, `Authenticator`, and `Settings`).
- **Crisp Readable Text**: Removed `QGraphicsDropShadowEffect` text ghosting and switched to a direct hard-edged container shadow.
- **High-Contrast Tab & Action Icons**: Dynamic contrast colors across active/inactive tabs and settings buttons.
