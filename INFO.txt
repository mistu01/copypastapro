Welcome to Mistus Copy Pasta (v1.3.0)!

Mistus Copy Pasta enhances Windows with advanced clipboard and security tools:
- 🟢 ZeBeyond Cyber-Emerald Midnight Theme: Sleek obsidian and neon mint/emerald UI aesthetics.
- 🎯 Input Box Quick Paste Dot: Subtle dot appears at active text boxes; hover over the dot to auto-expand recent pastable clips without clicking!
- ⌨️ Caret Quick Paste Hotkey: Press Alt + V (or Ctrl + Alt + V) anytime to instantly summon the quick paste menu right at your cursor.
- 📌 10 Pinned Slots on Top: Pin your top 10 clipboard items permanently.
- 🔑 Built-in 2FA TOTP: Live auto-detected two-factor codes with Windows DPAPI encryption.
- 🚀 Floating Bar: Draggable translucent pill on your desktop showing live 2FA and recent clips.
- 🗖 Detailed Flyout: Modern Fluent style clipboard flyout with live search.
- ⌨️ Intercepts Win + V, Ctrl + Shift + V, and Alt + V globally.

Setup will guide you through installing Mistus Copy Pasta on your computer.
