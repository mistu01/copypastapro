Welcome to Mistus Copy Pasta (v1.3.20)!

Mistus Copy Pasta enhances Windows with advanced clipboard, screenshot, and security tools:
- 🟢 ZeBeyond Cyber-Emerald Midnight Theme: Sleek obsidian and neon mint/emerald UI aesthetics.
- 📋 Modern Clipboard Manager: Fast Fluent flyout with real-time search, auto-paste, and history management (Win + V or Ctrl + Shift + V).
- 📌 10 Pinned Slots on Top: Lock your most important clippings and images permanently to top numbered slots.
- ⚡ Quick Copy on Selection: Highlight text on screen and press 'C' to copy directly without pressing Ctrl + C.
- 📸 Lightshot-Style Screenshot Suite: Dedicated PrintScreen (PrtScn) key capture with rectangle selection, pen/arrow/rectangle/text/highlighter annotations, and instant copy to clipboard & history.
- 🖼️ Multi-Format Image Support: Full copy & paste for all image formats (.png, .jpg, .bmp, .gif, .webp, .tiff, .ico) with thumbnail previews and dimensions badges.
- 🔑 Built-in 2FA TOTP: Live auto-detected two-factor codes with Windows DPAPI encryption and 1-click auto-paste.
- 🚀 Desktop Floating Island & Compact Pill: Collapsible dynamic island with live 2FA counter, quick-paste chips, and 1-click minimize to system tray.
- 🖱️ Smart System Tray Integration: Click tray icon to restore pill bar when minimized, or open full clipboard manager when active.

Setup will guide you through installing Mistus Copy Pasta on your computer.
